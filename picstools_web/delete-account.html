Account & Data Deletion

Under Google Play Store policies and international data privacy regulations (GDPR & CCPA), all PicsTools users have the right to permanently delete their account credentials, user profiles, and associated cloud synchronization data.

📱

Method 1: Instant Self-Service Deletion (In-App)

If you currently have the PicsTools app installed on your phone, you can delete your account immediately in under 10 seconds:

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Open the PicsTools app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap Privacy Policy.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap the red button: DELETE ACCOUNT & CLOUD DATA.
  5. Choose whether you wish to also delete local photos on this device (optional checkbox).
  6. Tap Delete Account to confirm.

What happens immediately: Your user document in Firebase Firestore is erased, your Google/Email authorization session is revoked, your Firebase account is permanently deleted, and the app resets to a guest session.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Category Data Type Action Upon Deletion
Account Profile Email, Display Name, User UID Permanently Deleted immediately from Firebase.
Cloud History Firestore history records Permanently Deleted from database.
Local Files On-device cached images in phone storage User Choice: Retained on device by default, or wiped if checkbox is selected.
In-App Purchases Purchase tokens Managed by Google Play Store. Subscriptions should be cancelled via Google Play subscriptions menu.
